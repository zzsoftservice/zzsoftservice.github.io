Privacy policy

Your water journal stays on your device.

Effective: October 21, 2026 · iPhone / Widget

What stays on your device

CupTally stores water entries, cup presets, personal goals, reminder plans, room arrangements and optional questionnaire answers (gender, age, height, current and target weight, goals and habits) locally. The app and Widget share this information through an iOS App Group.

Data collection

CupTally does not require an account or send your journal or questionnaire answers to the developer. The app has no advertising, tracking, third-party analytics, HealthKit access or developer-operated cloud sync. All features in version 1.0 are available free of charge.

Reminders and exports

Notifications are scheduled locally with your permission. You can disable them in the app or iOS Settings. CSV export occurs only when you request it, using the system share sheet. Copies you save or share are managed at their destination.

Manage and erase

You can edit your profile and journal in the app. Settings > Data > Erase journal data removes entries, cups, goals, growth, room arrangements and questionnaire answers, and disables reminders. Your unit preference, system permissions and previously exported files remain. Device backups and uninstallation follow iOS behavior.

Support and this website

If you email us, we receive the address, message and attachments you choose to send. They are used to answer your request and retained only as needed for support or legal obligations. Please avoid sending sensitive journal or health information. Contact zzsoftservice@outlook.com to request access to or deletion of support correspondence. This static website has no developer-set analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, forms or cookies. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process IP addresses and technical request information for security and operation under its own privacy policy.

Children and changes

CupTally is not designed specifically for children under 13. We do not knowingly request their personal information through support. Contact us if you believe a child has sent personal information. We update this page and its effective date if our practices change.
