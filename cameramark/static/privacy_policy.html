Privacy Policy

This policy explains how CameraMark handles information in the iOS and iPadOS app.

ProductSupportPurchase terms

Short version

CameraMark processes your photos and projects on your device. It does not require an account, operate a content server, sell personal information, show advertising, or track you across apps and websites.

Information handled on your device

When you choose to import or capture an image, CameraMark may read the image pixels and embedded metadata needed for the features you request. This can include camera and lens details, capture date, image dimensions and format, and GPS coordinates. Your projects may also contain your manual text, design recipes, logos, signatures, device profiles, brand kits, export presets, and export history.

These items are stored in the app’s local container. CameraMark accesses the Photos library, camera, or selected files only after your action and the applicable system permission.

Exports and sharing

You decide whether an export preserves supported metadata, keeps only copyright information, or removes metadata. GPS inclusion is a separate setting and is off by default. If you save to Photos, Files, or use the system share sheet, Apple and the destination app or service process that output under their own policies.

On-device analytics

Optional anonymous analytics are off by default. If enabled, CameraMark stores only aggregate event-name counters on your device. It does not store event parameters, photographs, EXIF data, file names, project names, account identifiers, advertising identifiers, or location in those counters, and it does not upload them. Resetting CameraMark defaults removes these local counters.

Purchases

Subscriptions and lifetime purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. CameraMark receives product and entitlement status needed to unlock features, but it does not receive your full payment-card information. Apple’s handling of purchase data is governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

Required system APIs

CameraMark uses UserDefaults to retain preferences and checks disk space before storage-intensive operations. These uses are declared in the app’s privacy manifest. The current release declares no collected data types and no tracking.

Retention and deletion

Projects remain on your device until you delete them. Deleted projects may remain in CameraMark’s Recently Deleted area until you permanently remove them. You can delete projects, clear cache data, reset product preferences, or delete the app to remove its local container. Copies that you exported are controlled from their destination.

Children

CameraMark is a general-audience creative tool and is not directed to children for the purpose of collecting personal information. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If CameraMark’s data practices change, this page and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before the new practice is introduced where required.
