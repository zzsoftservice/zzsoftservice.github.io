Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026 · Applies to DeskWidgets for macOS

DeskWidgets is provided by ZZSoft. This policy explains what information the app handles, when information leaves your Mac, and the choices available to you. Contact us at zzsoftservice@outlook.com.

In brief: DeskWidgets keeps its everyday configuration and content on your Mac by default. It does not require a DeskWidgets account and has no advertising or cross-app tracking SDK. Optional online features contact external providers. A version with iCloud enabled may also sync supported data through Apple. These exceptions mean that “nothing ever leaves your device” would not be accurate.

1. Information stored on your Mac

The app may store countdown titles and dates; display formats and total days; clock cities and time zones; timer and focus settings; notes, tasks and completion state; launcher destinations; file references; appearance settings; and cached widget content. The app and its widget extension share the data needed to display your widgets through a local App Group container.

File features keep names, paths and macOS security-scoped bookmarks for items you choose. These bookmarks let the app reopen authorized items; they are not uploads of the files. Photo-related information currently includes selected file metadata such as a filename and dimensions. System information such as CPU, memory, disk, network activity and battery readings is processed locally.

2. Optional macOS permissions

  • Calendars: after permission, the app reads relevant event titles and times to show your schedule. The app does not create or change calendar events.
  • Reminders: after permission, the app reads reminder information and can update completion when you request that action.
  • Files and folders: access is limited by the items you select and macOS authorization. Opening an item hands it to macOS or another application.

You can decline permission and still use unrelated features. Manage calendar and reminder permissions in System Settings → Privacy & Security. Remove file pins you no longer want to use. DeskWidgets does not request your Contacts or use automatic device-location access for weather.

3. Optional online information

When you configure or refresh an online source, your Mac connects directly to that provider over HTTPS. The provider receives the request, your IP address and ordinary connection information. DeskWidgets sends an application user-agent identifier, not a unique advertising identifier. Requests may include:

Feature / providerInformation sentPurpose
Weather / Open-MeteoThe latitude and longitude you enter and weather query optionsRetrieve weather for the selected location
RSS / your chosen feedThe feed URL, including any query values in itRetrieve feed entries
Online quotes / QuotableA request for a random quoteRetrieve a quote
Exchange rates / ExchangeRate-APIThe selected base currencyRetrieve conversion rates

Providers may keep connection logs under their own policies. As of this policy's update, Open-Meteo's policy describes logs that can contain IP addresses and geographic coordinates and are deleted after 90 days. Do not enter a precise private location unless you want it sent to that provider. Do not put passwords or confidential tokens in an RSS URL. You can avoid these requests by not configuring the online features or removing their sources. Previously fetched content may remain in a local cache.

Links, apps and shortcuts you open use the destination service's privacy practices. We do not control external providers or promise that their services are always available.

4. iCloud, where supported

The default local build does not enable iCloud sync. If your installed edition supports and enables it, supported configurations and content may be stored in your private CloudKit database under your Apple Account and synchronized between your devices. Apple operates this service under its privacy policy. DeskWidgets does not receive your Apple Account password. Local file access permissions are not transferable to another Mac simply by syncing a reference; you may need to authorize files again.

5. Support and this website

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever text or attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond, investigate issues and meet applicable obligations, not for advertising. Our email provider processes the message. Please redact private content and never send credentials.

This website uses GitHub Pages. GitHub may process IP addresses and technical request logs to operate and secure hosting, subject to the GitHub General Privacy Statement. These pages do not embed analytics, advertising scripts or tracking cookies.

6. Retention, deletion and your choices

Local content remains until you delete it or the relevant app data. Removing a widget alone does not delete its underlying saved content. Uninstalling a macOS app may leave its containers, shared data or backups behind. For complete removal, also manage those copies; contact support if you need help identifying DeskWidgets data safely.

If you use an iCloud-enabled edition, manage cloud copies through that edition's supported controls and Apple's storage tools; local deletion, cloud sync and backups may have different retention behavior. External providers control their own logs. Support correspondence is kept only as reasonably necessary to handle your request and applicable obligations. You may ask us to access, correct or delete information you sent to support; rights and exceptions depend on applicable law.

7. Security and sharing

DeskWidgets uses macOS sandbox permissions, local storage protections and HTTPS for its online requests. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising. We may share support information with service providers needed to handle it or when legally required. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure; use appropriate device protection and backups. Apple, hosting, email and online service providers may process data in other countries under their own policies.

8. Children and policy updates

DeskWidgets is a general-purpose productivity app, not a service designed to collect information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can address it. We may update this policy as the app changes; the revised date above identifies the current version. Material changes will be explained through the app or our support website as appropriate.
