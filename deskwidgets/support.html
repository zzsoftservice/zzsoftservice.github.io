Add your first widget

  1. Open DeskWidgets and configure the feature you want to use.
  2. Control-click your Mac desktop and choose Edit Widgets.
  3. Search for DeskWidgets, choose an available size, and drag the widget onto your desktop.
  4. Control-click the widget and choose its Edit option, if available, to select its content or display settings.

Desktop widgets require macOS 14 or later. The widget gallery can show both legacy and saved-countdown variants; check the description and select the matching saved content when editing.

Countdown formats

Days and hours measure the time until the deadline. Decimal values are truncated to two decimal places, not rounded up. Percentage is remaining time ÷ (total days × 24 hours) × 100; for example, 3.5 days remaining out of 7 is 50%. Months use an average month length, rather than calendar-month boundaries.

If two widgets differ, first check that they use the same saved countdown, deadline, display format and total days. Main-window settings and per-widget overrides are not always the same.

Refreshes and timers

macOS controls widget refresh budgets and may delay updates. A widget is not a continuously running copy of the app. Opening the app can refresh its data, but this does not guarantee a per-second update for every widget field. If a widget remains blank, open DeskWidgets once and check its selected content before removing and adding that widget again.

Permissions and online features

Calendar, reminders and file features need the corresponding macOS permission or a file you select. Manage calendar and reminder access in System Settings → Privacy & Security. Weather uses the coordinates you enter; weather, RSS, online quotes and exchange rates need internet access and may be unavailable if their providers cannot be reached.

iCloud

The default local edition does not enable iCloud sync. Availability depends on the installed edition and its signing capabilities. Do not rely on sync until your edition shows that it is supported and working; keep your own backup of important information.

Send a useful bug report

Include the app version, macOS version, affected widget and size, steps to reproduce, and a screenshot if helpful. Please remove personal notes, event details, file paths and account information first. Do not send passwords, API keys or other secrets.
