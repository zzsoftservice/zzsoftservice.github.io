After the repository owner publishes this directory, the base URL is
`https://zzsoftservice.github.io/deskwidgets/`. Local creation/commit does **not**
mean these URLs are live. No App Store badge is shown before a verified listing exists.

The app repository contains the matching fastlane metadata, screenshots,
validation command and release checklist under `docs/app-store/README.zh-CN.md`.
Keep both privacy translations and the App Store privacy answers consistent.

## Maintenance

- These pages have no external fonts, JavaScript, cookies or analytics. Shared styling is `assets/site.css`.
- Confirm the support mailbox and publisher identity before publication.
- The default Release build is local-only. Do not advertise iCloud as a released,
  tested feature until the shipping edition has been verified.
- Recheck online providers, retention and licensing when shipping. In particular,
  Open-Meteo discloses logs containing coordinates/IP and a 90-day deletion period;
  its free endpoint is restricted to non-commercial use. See its
  [terms and privacy](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms).
- Policy text reflects the inspected app behavior on 2026-09-22. Publisher review
  and any jurisdiction-specific legal review remain required before release.
- Do not edit another product's pages or the root homepage as part of this directory.
