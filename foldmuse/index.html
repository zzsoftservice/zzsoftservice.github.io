FoldMuse / Overview

A sheet of paper. Room to imagine.

FoldMuse is a quiet paper-folding studio. Slow down and see where the next fold takes you.

Follow your imagination

A fixed crease, a free-moving sheet. Move, rotate and scale your paper, then preview the next fold.

Open up on Duo

On a compatible iPhone Duo, turn on device following and let the paper move with the hinge.

Make the paper yours

Choose colors, textures or your own images for each side. Turn it over to see another possibility.

Keep every idea

Autosave your workspace, save separate artworks and share a transparent PNG or editable document.

For iPhone and iPad with iOS / iPadOS 27.0 or later. Hinge following requires a compatible iPhone Duo with iOS 27.1 or later. Available in Chinese and English.
