Data and limitations

Features rely on records and permissions available through Apple Health. Missing, delayed, incomplete, or inaccurate source data can affect displayed results. Health Helper does not measure body metrics itself. Trends and associations are not medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or promises of weight loss. It is not intended for emergencies.

Free and Pro

Core summaries, recent trends, weight logging, and 90-day CSV export are free. Optional Pro unlocks extended history, PDF and extended exports, and dashboard card customization. Full history means only the data Apple Health makes visible to this app on the current device.

Pro may be offered as an annual auto-renewing subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. Availability and localized price are shown in Apple's purchase confirmation. Lifetime is a non-consumable unlock, not a recurring subscription or a promise that an operating system or service will be supported forever.

Manage or cancel in your Apple Account subscription settings. Canceling or uninstalling the app does not retroactively undo completed purchases; uninstalling alone does not cancel a subscription.

Use Restore purchases with the purchasing Apple Account to restore eligible entitlements. Refund eligibility and handling follow Apple's policies and applicable law; visit Apple's Report a Problem service for requests.
