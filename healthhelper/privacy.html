Privacy policy

Your health. Your control.

Health Helper does not upload your HealthKit data to a developer-operated server.

Effective date: September 22, 2026 · Applies to Health Helper for iPhone/iPad, its widgets, its Watch companion, and this support website.

1. Who to contact

This app is provided by the Health Helper developer. For privacy questions, requests about support correspondence, or complaints, contact zzsoftservice@outlook.com. No account is required to use the app.

2. Health data and permissions

With your permission, the app reads weight, body fat, steps, active energy, exercise minutes, and workouts from Apple Health. Optional nutrition features read dietary energy, basal energy, protein, carbohydrates, and fat. Optional recovery features read sleep, resting heart rate, and heart rate variability (HRV). These optional categories are requested only when you enable their features.

Data is used on your device to display records, calculate trends and progress, and offer transparent, rule-based fitness suggestions. The app writes weight and optional body-fat measurements only when you choose to save them. It does not sell health information, use it for advertising, or send it to third-party analytics.

3. What stays on your devices

HealthKit remains the source of your health records. Loaded records and calculated trends are processed in app memory. Goals, units, appearance, reminders, and feature preferences are stored locally. Widgets receive only a small, protected App Group summary; Apple Watch receives a compact summary from its paired iPhone through Apple's WatchConnectivity. These companions do not query HealthKit directly.

The app has no developer-operated health-data backend, account system, advertising SDK, or third-party analytics SDK. It does not implement its own cloud health-data sync. Apple's Health/iCloud synchronization and system backups depend on your Apple settings and Apple's policies; this policy does not turn those system services off. Device protection also depends on your operating-system security and passcode settings.

4. Exports and reminders

Exports are generated locally only at your request. The system share sheet lets you choose a recipient or destination; the app does not automatically upload an export. Temporary working files are removed when the export flow finishes. Copies saved or shared by you remain under the chosen destination's control and must be removed there.

Optional notifications are scheduled locally and start disabled. They do not use a developer push server. You can disable them in the app or in system notification settings. Consider notification previews and visible widgets when using a shared device.

5. Purchases

Optional annual and lifetime Pro purchases use Apple's StoreKit. The app checks entitlement information supplied by Apple to unlock features; it does not receive your payment-card details or send receipts to a developer server. Apple processes payment and keeps its own transaction records under its policies.

6. Your choices, access, and deletion

You can change or revoke Health access in the Health app's profile / Apps and Services section (menu labels may vary by OS). HealthKit intentionally does not tell the app whether read access was refused, so absent data and restricted access share a neutral empty state.

Settings offers local preference reset and a separate confirmed action to delete weight and paired body-fat records created by this app. It does not modify or delete another source's records. Removing the app does not automatically erase records in Apple Health, exported copies, or Apple's purchase records. Manage those with the relevant app or provider. For a Watch or widget, remove the companion if you no longer want its last locally stored summary visible.

7. Support email and this website

If you email us, we receive what you send, including your email address and any attachments. Avoid sending health records unless you decide they are necessary. We use correspondence to handle your request, keep it only as needed for support and legal obligations, and can help with access or deletion requests at zzsoftservice@outlook.com. Email processing also involves your and our email providers; support correspondence is not uploaded by the app automatically.

This static website uses no analytics scripts, advertising, tracking pixels, forms, or cookies set by us. GitHub Pages hosts it and may log visitors' IP addresses and technical request information for security and service operation. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Following an external link or sending email is subject to that provider's policy.

8. Children, security, and policy changes

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly solicit their personal information through support. If you believe a child has sent personal information, contact us so we can address it.

We minimize the data we hold, but no device, export destination, or email system is completely risk-free. Material changes to this policy will be reflected here with a new effective date. New data uses may require updated permission or consent where applicable.

9. General fitness, not medicine

Health Helper is not a medical device. Trends and associations are informational, do not establish causation, and are not diagnosis, treatment, or medical advice.
