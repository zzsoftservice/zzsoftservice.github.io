Health Helper · iPhone & iPad

A clearer view of your progress.

Weight, activity, and workouts from Apple Health. One calm dashboard. No account. No ads.

Less digging. More clarity.

See your latest weigh-in alongside its smoothed trend, track your daily activity, and review the workouts behind your progress. Your health data is processed on your device, not a developer-operated health-data server.

Start with the essentials

The core dashboard, 7-day / 4-week / 12-week trends, weight logging, and recent 90-day CSV export are free.

Add context, on your terms

Optionally enable nutrition, sleep, resting-heart-rate and HRV insights, or local reminders that are off by default.

Go further with Pro

Optional annual or lifetime Pro adds 1-year / all-available history, PDF and extended exports, and dashboard customization. Prices appear in the app before purchase.

Built for your devices

Native iPhone and iPad layouts. Widgets offer a compact summary. A paired Apple Watch receives its summary from the iPhone.

Requires relevant records and permission in Apple Health. Available data varies by device and source. Health Helper is an independent app, not affiliated with or endorsed by Apple. General fitness information only; not a medical device or a weight-loss guarantee.
