Why is a card empty?

First check that Apple Health has records for the selected dates and that you enabled access to the relevant categories. HealthKit does not reveal whether read permission was denied, so the app cannot distinguish that case from no visible samples. Refresh Today after checking permissions. The app does not generate missing health measurements.

Why are the scale number and trend different?

The latest weight is an actual measurement. The trend smooths past measurements to make direction easier to see. A smooth line is not a new weigh-in, a forecast, or a medical conclusion.

What can I edit or delete?

Only weight and paired body-fat entries created by Health Helper. Use weight history to manage individual entries or Settings > Data controls for a separately confirmed bulk removal. Other sources' entries remain unchanged.

How do Pro and restoration work?

Pro is optional: annual subscription or one-time lifetime purchase. Open Settings > Pro > Restore purchases using the Apple Account that made the purchase. Manage annual subscriptions in your Apple Account subscription settings. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

For billing or refund requests, use Apple’s purchase support. The developer cannot access your payment-card details.

Where did my export go?

The system share sheet sends or saves the file to the destination you select. Look there for your copy. The app deletes its temporary export, not the copy you chose to keep. Recent CSV export is free; PDF and longer history require Pro.

Why is my widget or Watch out of date?

Open Health Helper and refresh its data. Widgets and Watch show a small last-generated summary and are subject to system refresh limits. Keep the iPhone and its paired Watch connected. iPad does not pair with Apple Watch.

Privacy and terms

Read the privacy policy or purchase and use terms. The app is an independent Apple Health companion for general fitness, not a medical service.
