StepVoyage · Your walking journey

Your steps. Your control.

Steps and questionnaire answers stay on your device. You choose when to export or share.

Effective: October 21, 2026 · Applies to the iPhone app, Widgets and this support website.

1. Who to contact

StepVoyage is provided by its developer. For privacy questions or support correspondence requests, contact zzsoftservice@outlook.com. No app account is required.

2. Apple Health and purpose

With your permission, StepVoyage reads only step counts from Apple Health to display walking progress, seven-day challenges, history and journey keepsakes. It never writes or deletes Apple Health records. HealthKit aggregates available sources. Apple intentionally does not disclose whether read access was denied, so a missing result is not treated as zero steps.

3. Data stored on your device

Step summaries, goal revisions, rounds, history, keepsakes and a local usage summary are stored on your device. The usage summary records activation and core-action days; it is not sent to us automatically. Questionnaire goals, motivation, gender, optional age, height, current and target weight, activity habits and walking preferences are saved in a separate protected local file. They are not written to Apple Health. Body measurements are personal records and do not determine medical recommendations.

4. Protection, widgets and backup

Health records and questionnaire files use iOS Data Protection and are excluded from device backup. The Widget receives only a protected App Group summary, not the questionnaire profile. There is no developer-operated health-data server, account system, advertising SDK, tracking SDK or automatic health-data upload. StepVoyage does not provide its own cloud sync or automatic cloud backup. Apple Health’s own synchronization remains governed by your Apple settings and Apple’s policies.

5. Sharing, exports and notifications

Sharing occurs only when you choose it in the system share sheet. Review cards hide detailed step totals by default. User-requested exports include walking records as JSON/CSV, questionnaire answers as JSON and a local usage summary. Temporary export files are protected and cleaned after use. Files you save or share remain under the chosen destination’s control. Optional reminders are scheduled locally after you enable them; there is no developer push server.

6. Your choices and deletion

Edit questionnaire answers or reminder preferences in Settings. You can revoke Health access through Apple Health. Clear local records removes the app’s local records, profile answers, keepsakes, Widget snapshot contents and reminders. It does not erase Apple Health originals or exports saved elsewhere. iOS can temporarily retain a previously rendered Widget image before refreshing. Export required records before uninstalling or changing phones; this version does not import an archive.

7. Support email and this website

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and attachments. Send only what is necessary; you do not need to send health records or questionnaire answers. Correspondence is used to handle your request and kept only as needed for support and applicable obligations. Ask us about access or deletion by email. This static website has no analytics scripts, tracking pixels, forms or cookies set by us. GitHub Pages may process IP addresses and technical request information to operate and secure hosting. See the GitHub Privacy Statement linked below. External links and email services have their own policies.

8. Children, security and changes

StepVoyage is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child sent personal information through support, contact us. Device and export security also depend on your operating-system protection and chosen destinations. We update the effective date when this policy materially changes.

9. General wellness

StepVoyage supports personal walking records and habits. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat conditions, prescribe activity, or guarantee weight loss. Choose goals suited to your circumstances.
