StepVoyage · Your walking journey

A little room for walking.

Set a daily step goal, build a seven-day habit and collect twelve keepsakes along your journey.

Your seven-day round

Choose a daily step goal and how many days you want to reach it in seven calendar days. Confirm the plan to start a round. A round keeps the time zone selected when it was created. Changing a daily goal applies from the next day and keeps earlier goal snapshots. When a round ends, review it and choose whether to start another.

Your walking profile

Optional personal questions help you record your preferences. You can skip age, height and weight questions, and edit answers in Settings. Body measurements do not determine step goals or medical advice. Changing profile preferences does not rewrite an active round.

Home Screen widgets

Touch and hold your Home Screen, choose Edit → Add Widget, and search for StepVoyage. Small and medium sizes are available. Widgets display the last protected summary; iOS controls refresh timing, so opening the app can show newer information.
