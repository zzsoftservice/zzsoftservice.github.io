The short version

GLP-1 Tracker is a personal health record app developed by PixelPlay. Your health records are processed and stored on your device. The app does not require an account, operate a health-data server, automatically sync records to the cloud, sell personal information, display advertising or track you across apps and websites.

Information stored on your device

The app can store the information you choose to enter: medication and prescribed treatment plans, schedules, actual doses and injection sites, skipped entries, inventory, weight and waist measurements, water, meals and nutrition, activity, symptoms, notes, selected photos, and the dates and time zones associated with those records. It also stores onboarding choices, display preferences and reminder settings.

These records are used to provide your journal, factual trends, reminders, reports, exports and backup features. They are not uploaded to PixelPlay. The current standard release does not include Apple Health import or access to HealthKit.

Photos and permissions

You select photos with the system photo picker. The app reads the items you choose; it does not request unrestricted access to your photo library. Imported copies are re-encoded to remove embedded metadata and stored in the protected app container. Original photos in your library are unchanged.

Notifications are optional and are scheduled locally on the device. Declining notification permission does not prevent manual recording. Notification wording and any visible health details depend on the reminder privacy setting you select.

Local storage and protection

The app applies iOS file protection to its health database and protected files, including database journal files, photos and temporary exports. The protected health-data directory is excluded from automatic iCloud Backup. The app does not use CloudKit or automatic iCloud synchronization for health records. Device security, including your passcode and access to the unlocked device, remains important.

The app obscures sensitive content in the task switcher. This does not prevent you or another person with access to your device from taking a screenshot or photographing the screen.

Exports, sharing and encrypted backups

Only when you request it, the app creates an export, PDF report or password-protected encrypted backup. You decide whether to share or save it through the system share sheet or file picker. A destination such as Files, iCloud Drive, an email app or another service may process or sync that copy under its own privacy policy. Ordinary reports and data exports are not the same as encrypted backups.

Backups may include your health records and imported photos. Keep both the backup and its password secure. PixelPlay does not receive your backup password and cannot recover it. Copies saved outside the app remain at their destination until you remove them there.

Diagnostics and support

The app does not automatically upload analytics or crash reports. If you choose to export diagnostics, the diagnostic file contains technical information such as app and operating-system versions and storage or permission status. It is designed to exclude record contents, medication names, doses, measurements, symptoms, notes, record identifiers and precise health timestamps. You choose whether to share that file.

If you email zzsoftservice@outlook.com, PixelPlay receives your email address and whatever information you include, such as your message and attachments. We use that information to respond to your request and resolve support issues. Email is handled by email service providers under their own policies. Do not include health records unless you decide they are necessary for your request.

Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the request, maintain a relevant support history or meet applicable legal obligations. You can ask us to delete support correspondence by contacting the same address, subject to any applicable retention requirement.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Local records remain until you remove them, use the app’s delete-all-data control or delete the app and its local container. Some record types offer a short undo period before permanent deletion. Offloading an app is different from deleting its data. Exported copies, backups and original photos must be deleted separately at their destinations.

PixelPlay cannot access, correct or recover the health records stored solely on your device. You can review and manage them in the app. For information you voluntarily send to support, contact us to request access, correction or deletion as applicable. We do not sell or share that information for advertising.

Adults and medical use

The app is intended for adults managing their own records. It is not designed for collecting information from children. It does not provide diagnosis, prescribing or treatment decisions and is not an emergency service.

This website and external links

These public product, support and policy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to provide and secure its hosting service. See GitHub’s privacy statement. We do not add analytics scripts, advertising or tracking cookies to these pages.

External sources opened from the app or this website are governed by their operators’ policies. Opening a link does not send your local health records to that site.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy if the app’s data practices change and provide any additional notice or choices required by applicable law. This policy applies to the standard GLP-1 Tracker 1.0 release.

For privacy questions, contact PixelPlay at zzsoftservice@outlook.com.
