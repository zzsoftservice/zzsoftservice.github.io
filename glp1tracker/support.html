Contact

Email zzsoftservice@outlook.com. Include your app version, iOS or iPadOS version and a brief description of the issue. Avoid sending personal health information.

Getting started

Choose Set up later to explore manual tracking, or enter the plan already prescribed by your clinician. Today provides quick recording actions; Journal shows saved entries; Trends summarizes the recorded facts.

Reminders

Check the reminder setting in your plan and the app’s notification permission in system Settings. Manual tracking remains available when reminders are disabled.

Export, backup and deletion

Use the data and privacy tools in Me to export records, create an encrypted backup or delete local data. Keep your backup password safe; we cannot recover it. Files saved outside the app must be managed separately.

Medical questions

Contact your clinician for questions about medication, dose, symptoms or treatment changes. The support mailbox is for app support and is not monitored as an emergency medical service.
