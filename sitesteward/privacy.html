SiteSteward

Your projects. Your choice to share.

How SiteSteward handles local project information, permissions and exports.

Scope and contact

Updated October 23, 2026. This policy covers SiteSteward, provided by LIPEI ZHANG, and this support website. For privacy questions email zzsoftservice@outlook.com.

No developer collection in the app

SiteSteward requires no account and includes no advertising, tracking, analytics SDK, online feedback endpoint or developer-operated project server. The app does not transmit project contents, location, device identifiers or usage history to the developer. Local diagnostics remain on the device unless you deliberately export and share them.

Information processed on your device

Projects may contain customer details, measurements, plans, photos/videos, quotations, financial records and inspection notes you enter or import. Originals can contain embedded capture/location metadata. The app stores project data, attachments, thumbnails, local recovery jobs and drafts in its local container. It does not upload these to a developer server.

Optional hardware and files

Camera access enables capture and AR. System photo and file pickers let you select existing files. Location supports current coordinates and direction assistance; manual coordinates work without it. Notifications support chosen reminders. Room scanning uses supported hardware and processes the captured room data locally.

Exports and third-party destinations

You choose when and where to export, back up or share. Customer reports use selected public fields. Customer media-copy exports remove supported photo/video metadata; selected documents and annotation text can still contain information you provided. Original export deliberately preserves source files and may include location metadata. Review the final file before sharing. System backups, iCloud file providers and third-party sharing destinations follow their own policies and your settings.

Manage, remove and recover

Use the recycle bin to recover or permanently remove project records. Files still referenced by another record or historical snapshot may be retained until no longer needed. Removing the app removes local app data; exported copies and system backups must be managed separately at their destinations. Export a checked backup before reinstalling.

Support and this website

Email you send to support is processed to answer your request and may include the details you voluntarily provide. This site has no application analytics or advertising scripts. Its hosting provider may process ordinary request/security logs under its own policy.

Changes

This page will be updated when information handling changes. No privacy claim here changes the need to review the recipient and contents of files you deliberately share.
