SiteSteward · 装修管家

Renovation, from site to handover.

SiteSteward keeps measurements, drawings, material estimates, quotes and inspection records in one local project.

A connected field workflow

Organize rooms and evidence, mark photos, calibrate drawings and retain measured values. Plan materials, paving and cutting with explicit inputs and assumptions.

Records you can trace

Keep production remeasurements and quote revisions, purchase batches, costs and cash, inspection rounds and outstanding handover work separate and traceable.

Free. Local. Yours.

No subscription, in-app purchases, trial or commercial quotas. No account is required. English and Simplified Chinese on iPhone and iPad. Export and share only when you choose.

Device support

iOS / iPadOS 17 or later. RoomPlan needs supported LiDAR hardware; manual drawing remains available. Camera, location and notifications are optional for the related features.

Before you build

Verify dimensions, material assumptions and fabrication rules with the responsible professional. Solar output is a direction and simplified-shadow reference, not an indoor sunlight-hour assessment.
