# FormTrace website

This directory owns FormTrace product, support and privacy pages. Canonical root: https://zzsoftservice.github.io/formtrace/ . English pages are at the root; Simplified Chinese pages are under `zh-Hans/`. There are no scripts, trackers, cookies or remote media.

The iOS app bundles an offline copy of the corresponding privacy content. Change the website policy first, then synchronize its content into `FormTrace/Resources/privacy_en.html` and `privacy_zh-Hans.html` in the app. Verify the text matches before release.

Scope commits to `formtrace/`. Preserve CameraMark and FrameTales compatibility links. The feature branch is not a production deployment; merge/publish and verify the public URLs before submitting App Store metadata.
