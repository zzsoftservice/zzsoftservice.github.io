Privacy policy

This policy explains how FormTrace handles information in the iPhone app. Effective October 17, 2026.

On-device records

FormTrace does not require an account and does not operate a server for your body photos or measurements. Photos, record dates, views, notes, manually entered measurements and editing instructions are stored in the app’s local container by default. Optional iCloud sync uses your existing Apple Account; no FormTrace account is created. We do not sell this information, include advertising SDKs, or track you across apps and websites.

Optional questionnaire and illustrations

You may provide your gender, age, height, current and target weight, goals, motivation, activity level, recording difficulties and preferred recording frequency. These optional answers remain in your protected local archive and are included in a complete encrypted export. They are uploaded to your private iCloud database only if you enable sync. They do not create measured check-ins or enable notifications. Gender selects a demonstration illustration and pose guide; it never changes your own photos. Edit answers in My profile, or clear the local archive to remove them.

Selected photos and metadata

When you choose a photo, FormTrace reads the delivered file, its image dimensions, format, orientation and available capture date. The file is kept unchanged, so embedded metadata may include location. Camera capture is used only when you request it and grant system permission. Imported iCloud Photos items may be downloaded by Apple; this is separate from FormTrace uploading an archive.

Optional iCloud sync

Sync is off by default. If you enable it in Settings, original photos (including metadata such as location), record dates and views, notes, measurements, edits and questionnaire answers are uploaded to the private CloudKit database associated with your Apple Account. Apple operates this service under its iCloud terms and privacy policy; we do not operate a separate data server. The app uses encrypted CloudKit fields for content and CloudKit assets for original files. Sync uses your iCloud storage and network connection. Display preferences, device authentication and notification settings remain device-specific. Sync runs while the app is open and unlocked and pauses in the background. Account changes pause synchronization; the archive stays bound to the original account.

Protection and automatic backup

The app applies iOS file protection to its archive, database, working files and exports. Its local photo and measurement archive is excluded from automatic system backup. Optional iCloud sync is separate from system backup and does not replace an independently saved password-encrypted archive. Optional device authentication uses Apple’s LocalAuthentication interface; FormTrace does not receive Face ID biometric templates or your device passcode. A privacy cover conceals app content when it is inactive or in the background.

User-controlled export and migration

You can export original files, comparison JPGs and complete password-encrypted .formtrace archives. Comparison JPGs do not copy source GPS metadata. Originals and complete archives preserve the original files and metadata. Your archive password is not sent to us and is not recoverable by us. Save both the file and its password before changing phones or deleting the app.

Files, Photos and sharing

When you save to Photos, choose a Files location, or share with another app, Apple or the selected provider may store or synchronize that external copy according to its own settings and policies. FormTrace cannot revoke copies already saved or shared outside its container.

Reminders and preferences

Optional reminders are scheduled locally and do not include body values. The app stores choices such as display units, appearance, reminder time and access protection in its own preferences. It checks free disk space before file-intensive operations. These uses are declared in its privacy manifest.

Logs and analytics

The first release has no advertising or analytics SDK and does not upload usage counters. Operational logs omit photos, body values, source filenames, GPS and precise photo dates. If you email support, we receive the information you choose to include and use it to answer your request. Do not attach sensitive photos unless you choose to do so.

Deletion

Deleting a record while sync is enabled sends its deletion to enabled devices. Incoming changes that would remove or replace a local original wait for a separate review on that device; until approved, the current local archive remains readable and exportable. Concurrent edits and deletions are retained for review, and the edit remains visible until resolved. Unreferenced original files may remain in iCloud until the cloud archive is removed. Turning sync off keeps both local and cloud data. To remove all FormTrace cloud records, conflicts and retained photo files, first turn off sync on every device, then use Settings → iCloud sync → Remove FormTrace data from iCloud and confirm. Local copies stay on each device. Clear the local archive separately to remove this device’s photos, measurements, edits, questionnaire and sync cache. Re-enabling sync merges local and cloud content again; remaining local data can be uploaded again. Interrupted file cleanup is retried when the app next checks its archive. Deleting the app removes its local container. External exports and selected providers’ copies must be deleted at their destination.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy and the App Store privacy information if actual data practices change. Privacy questions can be sent to zzsoftservice@outlook.com.
