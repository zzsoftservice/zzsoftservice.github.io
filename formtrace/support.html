For a question or a problem, contact zzsoftservice@outlook.com. Include your app version, iOS version, device model and the steps that led to the issue. You do not need to send private body photos.

Contact support

Changing phones or deleting the app

First export a complete archive from Settings → Privacy & archive. Save the .formtrace file somewhere safe, and keep its password separately. Automatic system backup excludes the app’s body-photo archive.

Restoring a complete archive

On the new installation, choose Restore archive and provide the file and password. Restoration accepts an empty local archive only. Back up existing records before choosing to clear them. A forgotten archive password cannot be recovered by us.

Missing or incorrect dates

Review dates and views during import. Dates without photo metadata need your confirmation; you can correct a record’s date later.

Camera or Photos permissions

Photos import uses the system picker for only the items you choose. If camera access is off, you can still import, view and compare. Saving a report to Photos asks for add-only access.

What measurements mean

Weight and circumferences are entered by you. Labels point to information you provide; they do not measure a distance in the photograph.

Export and privacy

Comparison JPGs do not copy source GPS metadata. Original files and complete archives preserve the imported files and their metadata. Check manual covers and displayed fields before sharing.
